User Data Deletion

Last updated: 15 October 2026

If you have authorized the Auto Card Deck application through Facebook or Instagram, you can request deletion of information associated with that authorization.

Option 1: Revoke access through Meta

  1. Open your Facebook or Meta account settings.
  2. Go to the section that manages apps, websites, business integrations, or connected experiences.
  3. Find Auto Card Deck.
  4. Remove or revoke the application's access.

Revoking access prevents the application from continuing to use the permissions previously granted.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

Send an email to nemedria@gmail.com with the subject:

Data Deletion Request – Auto Card Deck

Include enough information to identify the connected Meta account, Facebook Page, or Instagram account. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What happens next

We will verify the request and delete information controlled by Auto Card Deck that is no longer required for legal, security, or operational reasons. We aim to process valid requests within 30 days.

Information controlled by Meta

Content and information stored directly by Facebook or Instagram are controlled by Meta and may need to be managed or deleted using Meta's own account and privacy controls.

Contact: nemedria@gmail.com
