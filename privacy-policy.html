Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Auto Card Deck application processes information when it is used with Meta products such as Facebook and Instagram.

1. Purpose of the application

Auto Card Deck is used to manage and publish automotive-related content to authorized Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts. The application is not intended to sell personal information or build advertising profiles.

2. Information we may process

When an authorized administrator connects the application to Meta, the application may process only the information required to operate the integration, including:

  • Meta user or administrator identifiers needed for authentication.
  • Facebook Page and Instagram account identifiers.
  • Access tokens or authorization credentials provided by Meta.
  • Content, captions, images and publication metadata submitted for publishing or management.
  • Technical logs needed to diagnose errors and maintain the integration.

3. How we use information

  • To authenticate authorized administrators.
  • To publish and manage content on authorized Facebook and Instagram accounts.
  • To maintain the security and reliability of the integration.
  • To diagnose technical problems.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Information is not shared with third parties except where necessary to operate the service, comply with law, protect security, or interact with Meta's platforms according to their terms.

5. Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the integration, satisfy legal obligations, resolve disputes, or protect the service. Access credentials may be removed when authorization is revoked or when they are no longer required.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect credentials and other information processed by the application. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and rights

You may revoke the application's access at any time through your Meta account or business settings. You may also request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with the application by contacting us.

8. Data deletion

Instructions for requesting deletion of data are available on our User Data Deletion page.

9. International users

Where applicable, users may have rights under data-protection laws such as the GDPR. Requests can be sent to the contact address below.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, Meta requirements, or applicable laws change. The latest revision date will appear at the top of this page.
